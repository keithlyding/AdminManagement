# Using it on the iPad (and phone)

## The Microsoft Lists app

Get **Microsoft Lists** from the App Store and sign in with your work account. It has
supported iPad since 2021 ([Microsoft announcement](https://techcommunity.microsoft.com/blog/microsoft_365blog/ipad-support-now-available-in-microsoft-lists-for-ios/2281161)).
Your *Action Items* list appears under Recent or Favorites; star it so it stays on top.
Pick the **My Open Tasks** view.

Not sure: the red/yellow row colors and the bold red scores come from SharePoint's
formatting rules, which the mobile app may show only partly. Everything else (views,
sorting, adding and editing) works the same.

If your company blocks the Lists app, open the list in **Safari**, then **Share > Add to
Home Screen**: it gets its own icon and opens full screen.

## Writing with the Pencil

iPadOS's **Scribble** turns Apple Pencil handwriting into typed text in any text box,
including the Lists app's fields. Write straight into *Task* or *Description*. (Settings >
Apple Pencil > Scribble, if it's off.)

## In a meeting

Keep taking notes in OneNote. When something is an action item, either:
- add it right away in Lists (**+ New**, Source = Meeting), or
- star or circle it in OneNote and move it over at the end of the day, when the
  [Daily review](../flows/04-daily-review.md) item comes up.
